Доростеф (Dorostef)

A free educational app in Serbian for children aged about 7–13.

What it is

Short lessons followed by quizzes, in four subjects:

  • General knowledge
  • Good manners (bonton)
  • Mathematics
  • Serbian language

Children earn stars as they progress. Parents can set their own rewards in a parent-only area.

The app can be read in Cyrillic or Latin script, and in ekavian or ijekavian.

Privacy

  • Free, with no ads and no purchases.
  • No accounts. Works offline.
  • Does not collect or share personal data. Progress stays on the device.
