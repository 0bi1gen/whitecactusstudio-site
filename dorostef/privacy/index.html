Privacy Policy — Доростеф (Dorostef)

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Доростеф (“the app”) is a free educational app for children, made by White Cactus Studio (Bojan Malic, individual), Grevlingstien 8, 1555 Son, Norway (“we”, “us”).

The short version

The app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. Everything you or your child enter or achieve in the app stays on the device. We have no servers, no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app has no in-app purchases.

What the app stores on the device

To work, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • Lesson progress, quiz scores and star ratings
  • A log of which quiz questions were answered correctly or incorrectly (used to build the “review” list)
  • Text a child types into written exercises
  • Reward text a parent types into the parent-only rewards settings
  • Settings: script (Cyrillic/Latin), dialect (ekavian/ijekavian), haptics, pass threshold

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, access it or recover it. Because it is stored only on the device, deleting the app, or using the reset option in the app’s settings, removes it permanently. It may be included in your phone’s own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup), which is controlled by your device settings and your platform provider, not by us.

What the app does not do

  • It does not require an account or sign-in.
  • It does not collect names, email addresses, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, advertising identifiers or device identifiers.
  • It does not contain advertising or third-party analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.
  • It does not make network requests for its own functionality. All lessons, fonts and images are bundled in the app.
  • It does not offer purchases of any kind.

Children

The app is designed for children (roughly ages 7–13). Because we collect no personal data, there is no child data for us to use, share or sell. Parent-only areas (such as the rewards settings) are protected by a simple parental gate.

Third parties

Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) distribute the app and may process data about your download and device under their own privacy policies. We receive none of that personal data. The app contains no third-party software that sends data off the device.

This website

This website is static: no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts, fonts or embeds. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log technical data about visits, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or use that data.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you or your child, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you are in the EEA/Norway you still have the rights granted by the GDPR, and you may contact us or the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet) with any concern.

Changes

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy (for example adding any online feature), we will update this policy and the app listing before the change is released.
